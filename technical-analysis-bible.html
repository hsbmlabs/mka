The Complete Technical Analysis Guide

Chart Patterns

Head and Shoulders

A reversal pattern consisting of three peaks, with the middle peak (head) being higher than the two surrounding peaks (shoulders). Signals a potential trend reversal from bullish to bearish.

  • Entry Point: Break of neckline
  • Stop Loss: Above head
  • Target: Distance from head to neckline

Double Bottom

A bullish reversal pattern forming a "W" shape. Shows price reaching a support level twice before breaking upward.

  • Entry Point: Break of resistance
  • Stop Loss: Below second bottom
  • Target: Height of pattern

Technical Indicators

Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)

A trend-following momentum indicator showing the relationship between two moving averages of an asset's price.

  • Signal Line: 9-period EMA of MACD
  • MACD Line: 12-period EMA - 26-period EMA
  • Histogram: MACD Line - Signal Line

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

Momentum oscillator measuring the speed and magnitude of recent price changes to evaluate overbought or oversold conditions.

  • Overbought: Above 70
  • Oversold: Below 30
  • Divergence: Price/RSI mismatch

Advanced Concepts

Fibonacci Retracement

Key levels: 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, and 78.6% of a price move, used to identify potential support/resistance levels.

Elliott Wave Theory

Market cycles consist of 5 waves in the direction of the main trend (impulse waves) and 3 waves in the correction (corrective waves).
